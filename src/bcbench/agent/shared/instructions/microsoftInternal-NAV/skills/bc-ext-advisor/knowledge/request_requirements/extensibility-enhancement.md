# Extensibility Enhancement Requirements

Applies to `extensibility-enhancement`, in addition to the general requirements. Covers extensibility improvements that are not events, visibility changes, or enums.

## Requirements
- **Proposed enhancement** — *Mandatory.* Describe the exact change: the object, member, or field affected, with before/after snippets or a precise design description. Avoid vague descriptions.
- **Technical justification** — *Mandatory.* Explain the current limitation and the architectural or performance benefit, or the specific scenario where today's design fails. Avoid generic claims such as "this would improve extensibility".
- **Non-breaking confirmation** — *Mandatory.* Confirm the enhancement preserves every public contract, or identify precisely what would change.
